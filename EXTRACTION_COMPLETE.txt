💡 KEY ALGORITHMS INCLUDED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Payroll Calculation:
  Total Pay = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay + Night Premium 
              + Weekend Premium + Holiday Premium

  Where:
  - Regular Pay = Regular Hours × Hourly Rate
  - Overtime Pay = OT Hours × Hourly Rate × 1.5
  - Night Premium = Night Hours × Hourly Rate × (1.5 - 1)
  - Weekend Premium = Total Hours × Hourly Rate × (1.25 - 1)
  - Holiday Premium = Total Hours × Hourly Rate × (2.0 - 1)

  Rules:
  - Overtime kicks in after 40 hours/week
  - Night hours: 10 PM - 6 AM
  - Weekend: Saturday or Sunday
  - Premiums stack intelligently (holiday takes precedence)
  - Break time is deducted from total hours
